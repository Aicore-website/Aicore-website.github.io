Privacy Policy

AI CORE Backup is used only by its owner.

  • It requests only the Google Drive "drive.file" permission: it can see and change only the files it creates itself.
  • Files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. Neither Google nor anyone else can read them without the owner's password.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or sent to any third party.
  • Access can be removed at any time from Google Account > Security > Third-party connections.
